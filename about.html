Meet Your Neurosurgeon

Dr. Nihal Ahemad

Consultant – Epilepsy & Functional Neurosurgeon

Dr. Nihal Ahemad is a Consultant Epilepsy and Functional Neurosurgeon at SPARSH Hospital, Bengaluru, with over 10 years of professional practice. His clinical expertise focuses on epilepsy surgery, functional neurosurgery, deep brain stimulation and advanced neuromodulation.

He holds an MBBS, MS in General Surgery and M.Ch in Neurosurgery, followed by specialised fellowship training in Epilepsy and Functional Neurosurgery, and Stereotaxy and Pain. His professional journey includes clinical and academic exposure at AIIMS, New Delhi, and Uniklinik Köln, Germany.

His areas of expertise also include movement disorders, stereotactic neurosurgery, pain management and spine surgery. His approach emphasises careful clinical evaluation, clear communication and treatment planning based on individual patient needs.
