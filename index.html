About Your Neurosurgeon

Expertise that advances brain and neurological care.

Practice
Experienced. Specialised. Patient-Focused.

Advanced neurosurgical expertise with a patient-centred approach.

Dr. Nihal Ahemad is a Consultant Epilepsy and Functional Neurosurgeon at SPARSH Hospital, Bengaluru. With over 10 years of professional practice, he specialises in epilepsy surgery, functional neurosurgery and advanced neuromodulation techniques.

His professional training includes specialised fellowships in Epilepsy and Functional Neurosurgery, with advanced clinical exposure at AIIMS, New Delhi, and Uniklinik Köln, Germany. His expertise includes Deep Brain Stimulation (DBS), movement disorders, stereotactic procedures and minimally invasive spine surgery.
