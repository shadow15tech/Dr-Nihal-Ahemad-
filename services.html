Specialised Neurosurgical Care

Specialised expertise. Individualised treatment approaches.

01

Epilepsy Surgery

Epilepsy surgery is a specialised treatment option considered for selected patients whose seizures remain difficult to control despite appropriate medical treatment. Dr. Nihal Ahemad specialises in epilepsy surgery, with an approach centred on detailed evaluation and individual clinical findings.

  • Assessment of seizure history and previous treatment
  • Review of relevant neurological investigations
  • Evaluation of suitability for epilepsy surgery
  • Individualised surgical planning when indicated
02

Functional Neurosurgery

Functional neurosurgery focuses on specialised surgical approaches to neurological conditions affecting the functioning of the nervous system. It includes advanced techniques used in the management of selected movement disorders and other neurological conditions.

  • Specialised neurological evaluation
  • Assessment of functional neurological conditions
  • Evaluation for appropriate neuromodulation approaches
  • Individualised functional neurosurgical planning
03

Deep Brain Stimulation (DBS)

Deep Brain Stimulation is an advanced neuromodulation technique involving the placement of specialised electrodes in targeted areas of the brain. Controlled electrical stimulation is used to influence specific neural circuits in carefully selected patients with neurological and movement disorders.

  • Assessment for DBS suitability
  • Evaluation of movement-related neurological conditions
  • Specialised functional neurosurgical planning
  • Individualised consideration of neuromodulation therapy
04

Movement Disorders

Movement disorders can affect the speed, control and coordination of voluntary movements. Specialised assessment helps understand the underlying neurological condition and determine whether functional neurosurgical treatment or neuromodulation may be appropriate.

  • Evaluation of movement-related symptoms
  • Clinical assessment of neurological conditions
  • Consideration of Deep Brain Stimulation when appropriate
  • Individualised treatment planning
05

Stereotactic Neurosurgery & Pain Management

Stereotactic neurosurgery uses specialised techniques to accurately plan and target specific areas of the nervous system. Dr. Nihal Ahemad also has specialised fellowship training in Stereotaxy and Pain, supporting his expertise in functional neurosurgery and pain-related interventions.

  • Specialised stereotactic neurosurgical expertise
  • Precision-oriented treatment planning
  • Clinical evaluation for pain-related neurosurgical care
  • Individualised assessment of appropriate interventions
06

Spine Surgery

Spine surgery involves the neurosurgical evaluation and management of selected conditions affecting the spinal structures. Treatment decisions depend on clinical findings, symptoms and the individual patient's requirements.

  • Clinical evaluation of spinal conditions
  • Review of relevant medical investigations
  • Assessment of appropriate surgical options
  • Individualised neurosurgical treatment planning
